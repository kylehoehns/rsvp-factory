# Promotion skips parties that don't fit

When seats open at a full event, the earliest waitlisted RSVP *whose party fits the open seats* is promoted, even if a larger party is ahead of it in line; the larger party keeps its place at the head. We chose this over strict first-in-line because strict order leaves seats empty at a sold-out event until enough open up for the party at the head, which members experience as the app being broken. The cost is that a large party can wait longer than a small one that joined the waitlist after it.
